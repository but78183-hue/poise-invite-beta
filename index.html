PRIVATE, INVITATION-ONLY BETA

A clearer control layer for the exchange account you already own.

POiSE is a non-custodial product concept for self-directed exchange users. This preview is deliberately limited: it does not open accounts, collect API keys, connect exchanges, or enable automated trading.

BETA SCOPE

A small, reviewed starting point.

Invitation, not open enrollment

The first cohort is manually reviewed. This page is an availability overview, not an account-registration or exchange-binding flow.

Product learning, not a return claim

Early feedback helps improve clarity, risk controls, and support. It does not create a promise about profit, yield, performance, or any financial outcome.

Human control remains visible

The intended product direction keeps permissions and stop paths visible to the account owner. This static preview does not activate any product function.

YOUR CONTROL

Non-custodial by position, cautious by default.

  • Funds stay with the user. POiSE is not a wallet, bank, or custodian.
  • No withdrawal permission. Withdrawal authority is outside the intended path.
  • No live connection here. This page has no API-binding, execution, or account-data capability.
  • No exchange endorsement asserted. POiSE is an independent product concept.
